# Interface: RadialForceOptions

> **radius**: `number`

Target circle radius.

***

### strength?

> `optional` **strength?**: `number`

`radial.strength(s)`.

***

### x?

> `optional` **x?**: `number`

Circle center x.

***

### y?

> `optional` **y?**: `number`

Circle center y.
